Resets the simulation, then sends N = 100 requests in a fixed, seeded order: 70 valid top-ups across the three accounts and 6 each of replay (409), missing/expired/forged token (401), unconfirmed payment (400), unknown customer (404) and wrong meter (403). It then checks every balance, fires 20 simultaneous copies of one transaction, and verifies the audit chain. Use the Localhost network profile for timing.
